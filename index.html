ZH-L16C Planner

Bühlmann ZH-L16C with gradient factors, for open circuit, CCR and CCR with open-circuit bailout. Every plan recomputes in your browser as you change the dive.

How close each compartment is to its M-value at the moment you reach the surface, as a gradient factor. The leading compartment is the highest bar.

How the plan is made

  • ZH-L16C with 16 compartments (compartment 1b), Schreiner equation while moving, exact loading at constant depth. Salt water, 10 m per bar.
  • CCR loop: diluent inert gas at ambient pressure minus water vapour minus setpoint.
  • ppO2 is shown exactly and flagged above 1.6 bar, except pure oxygen at the standard 6 m stop (1.61).
  • END: equivalent narcotic depth with oxygen and nitrogen narcotic and helium not, against air; in CCR from the actual loop gas. Rounded to the nearest metre.
  • No-deco limit: if a direct ascent keeps every compartment within GF high on surfacing, the plan has no deco stops. The optional safety stop is added after that check.
  • First stop: GF-low ceiling at the end of the bottom, rounded up to the stop grid, moved one stop deeper if the ascent would already violate it (Baker's projected ascent).
  • Each stop is held to the next whole minute, then a minute at a time until the ceiling at the next stop's GF clears the next stop. GF rises linearly with depth from GF low at the first stop to GF high at the surface.
  • Open circuit and bailout: the richest gas within its switch depth is used. On the way to the first stop the switch happens at the switch depth; after that, on arrival at a stop.
