ZH-L16C Planner

Bühlmann ZH-L16C with gradient factors, for open circuit, CCR and CCR with open-circuit bailout. Every plan recomputes in your browser as you change the dive.

How the plan is made

  • ZH-L16C with 16 compartments (compartment 1b), Schreiner equation while moving, exact loading at constant depth. Salt water, 10 m per bar.
  • CCR loop: diluent inert gas at ambient pressure minus water vapour minus setpoint.
  • ppO2 is shown exactly and flagged above 1.6 bar, except pure oxygen at the standard 6 m stop (1.61).
  • Gas: RMV times ambient pressure over time, in surface litres. Bottom RMV for the descent, bottom, ascent and first stop, deco RMV after. Switch depths and gas volumes use 1 bar plus 1 bar per 10.08 m.
  • END: equivalent narcotic depth with oxygen and nitrogen narcotic and helium not, against air; in CCR from the actual loop gas. Rounded to the nearest metre.
  • Levels: level 1 time includes the descent; an added level's time is the time at depth, with the travel added. OC levels can each have their own gas; CCR stays at the bottom setpoint. Moving up to a shallower level adds stops wherever needed so the GF-low ceiling is never crossed; deco is planned from the end of the last level.
  • Last-stop GF (optional): the stops follow the GF low to GF high line, but the last stop is held until surfacing would leave every compartment at or below this GF.
  • CCR diluent changes (optional): on the ascent each listed diluent is used from its depth up, switched like an OC gas (at the depth on the way to the first stop, then on arrival at a stop). The setpoint stays as set.
  • CCR oxygen at the last stop (optional, either or both): the last minutes of the loop's last stop on open-circuit oxygen, and extra time on oxygen after the loop clears. The ascent from the last stop is then on oxygen.
  • Travel gas (OC, optional): the descent is on the travel gas down to its switch depth (default: its MOD at ppO2 1.4), then on the bottom gas.
  • Gas alerts (warnings only): ppO2 above 1.4 bar in the working part of the dive (descent, travel gas, bottom, levels) or above 1.6 bar during the deco; END deeper than 40 m anywhere; for CCR, a diluent flush above 1.4 bar at a level depth, or above 1.6 bar at a diluent change.
  • No-deco limit: if a direct ascent keeps every compartment within GF high on surfacing, the plan has no deco stops. The optional safety stop is added after that check.
  • First stop: GF-low ceiling at the end of the bottom, rounded up to the stop grid, moved one stop deeper if the ascent would already violate it (Baker's projected ascent).
  • Each stop is held to the next whole minute, then a minute at a time until the ceiling at the next stop's GF is within 1 cm of the next stop. GF rises linearly with depth from GF low at the first stop to GF high at the surface.
  • Open circuit and bailout: the richest gas within its switch depth is used. On the way to the first stop the switch happens at the switch depth; after that, on arrival at a stop.
